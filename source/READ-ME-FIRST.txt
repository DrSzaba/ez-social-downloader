EZ Social Downloader 1.2.0  -  El Szaba
=======================================

INSTALL
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click  EZ-Social-Downloader-Setup.exe
3. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box:
   click "More info", then click "Run anyway".
4. Click Install. A desktop icon is created. Done.

Everything is included (download engine, video converter).
No Python, no admin rights, nothing else to install.

USE
1. Copy a video link (Share > Copy link) from TikTok, Instagram,
   Facebook, YouTube, YouTube Shorts or X.
2. Click Paste (you can paste many links, one per line).
3. Pick what you want with the round buttons next to "Get:":
     Video (MP4) / Audio (MP3) / Transcript - lyrics / Video + transcript
4. Click DOWNLOAD. Files are saved to  Videos\EZ Downloads
   in a folder for each site.

NEW IN 1.2.0
- Transcripts / lyrics: get just the words of a TikTok, YouTube,
  Instagram, Facebook or X video - no video needed. They are saved in
  Videos\EZ Downloads\Transcripts as a plain .txt file (plus a timed
  .vtt or .srt caption file). English and Spanish captions are picked
  first; if there are none, whatever language the video has.
  Only videos that have captions have a transcript (the creator turned
  captions on, or the site made automatic ones).
- New rounded buttons and a cleaner look.
- Newest download engine included.

IF SOMETHING FAILS
- Click "Update engine" and try again (sites change often).
- A post says "Log in for access" (TikTok 18+, private posts):
  click "Log in" under that site in the app, log in, click DONE.
  A green check means you're logged in. Then download again.

UNINSTALL: Settings > Apps > EZ Social Downloader > Uninstall.
