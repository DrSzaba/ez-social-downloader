How it works

Copy the link

In the app or website, tap Share → Copy link on the video you want.

Paste it

Click Paste in EZ Social Downloader. Got a bunch? Paste them all, one per line.

Press DOWNLOAD

Your videos land in Videos\EZ Downloads, with a folder for each site.

How to install

  1. Click Download for Windows above and save the zip file.
  2. Right-click the zip → Extract All, then double-click EZ-Social-Downloader-Setup.exe.
  3. Click Install. A Desktop icon is made for you. Done.
Seeing a blue "Windows protected your PC" box? That's Windows being careful with any new program from a small maker. Click More info, then Run anyway. Everything is inside the setup (download engine and video converter) — no Python, no admin password, nothing else to install.

Questions

A video won't download. What do I do?

Click Update engine in the app and try again. Sites change how they work often, and the update fixes most problems in a few seconds.

It says "Log in for access".

That post is private or age-restricted. Click Log in under that site in the app, sign in, and click DONE. When you see the green check, download again.

Where do my videos go?

To Videos\EZ Downloads, sorted into a folder for each site.

Can I repost the videos I download?

Only videos you made yourself, or ones you have the creator's permission to use. Saving a video doesn't give you the rights to it.

Does it work on a phone or Mac?

Not yet — it's for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

How do I remove it?

Settings → Apps → EZ Social Downloader → Uninstall.
